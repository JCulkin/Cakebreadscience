# Worksheets and booklets

## Role in the site learning strategy & research principles
- Own **Layer 4 (written exam practice — assembling recalled facts into coherent 2–6 mark answers)**, grounded in **Faded Worked-Example Scaffolding** (Sweller; Pearson & Gallagher: worked first rows, word banks on KS3/SA, progressive ramp `recall → single-step → multi-step → unfamiliar`) and **Attempt-First Self-Explanation** (students write answers first, then mark in another colour pen using the mark scheme).
- **Three distinct Layer-4 formats (do not blur them):**
  - **Worksheets (`worksheets/`, 2–4 sides):** Single-lesson / sub-topic scaffolded classwork & homework (KS3 + KS4 + Single Award twins), used *during* a topic right after a projector lesson.
  - **Topic Booklets (`booklets/<subject>/<slug>.html` + `…_ms.html`, 10–14 pages):** Whole-topic printed workbooks (class packs, cover work, pre-exam paper revision) with compact `Key ideas` refreshers fading into unguided `★ Exam-style` synthesis. Cover must carry `.cv-title` + `.cv-sub` starting with `Spec X.Y–Z.W` so `node tools/spec/build_index.js` indexes them onto Spec Trackers and Study Guides automatically. Load `booklets/assets/booklet.js` + `/js/home-link.js`; list on `booklets/index.html`.
  - **6-Marker Long-Answer Booklets (`interactive/physics-long-answer/`):** Extended 4–6 mark exam technique with online **black-then-purple** self-marking and a `learned.html` gap bank (also printable).
- **Concrete research checklist for every worksheet + mark scheme:**
  - **Worked example + fading:** worked first row (`.ex` italic blue) on KS3/SA/drill tables; worked model or scaffolded starter before unguided KS4 explanations/calculations.
  - **Refutation:** at least one *"Spot the mistake" / "A student wrote… explain why this is wrong"* (KS4) or guided T/F-and-fix / circle-the-correction check (KS3/SA) targeting a real IGCSE misconception.
  - **Examiner Key Words (`/js/key-words.js`):** reinforce the exact mark-earning terms for the sheet's spec points; avoid banned/vague exam phrasing (`amount of` for measurable quantities, `reliable` alone, `germs`, `enzymes are killed` outside refutation).
  - **Attempt-first self-marking (mark schemes):** `.howto` banner at the top (*mark in a different colour, add missing key words*), explicit bulleted credit points with **bolded key words** on extended answers, and **≥2 targeted `.tip` misconception notes** explaining *why* common slips lose marks.
  - **Cross-layer glue (screen-only):** `worksheets/science.html` links to Topic Booklets and 6-Marker Booklets; individual sheets inject screen-only (`@media print { display: none }`) Flashcards / Practice / Revision Notes links via `worksheets/sheet-links.js`.

## Products
- **Worksheet** (KS4 and KS3 sets) and **Booklet** (whole topic, 10–14 pages). Built in teaching order
  (the user keeps a log of what each group has been taught in his Claude project; ask him if unsure).
- Multiple worksheets can cover the same spec point (numbered in sequence, e.g. `3b2` + `3b3`, `1` + `1b`). No separate "Challenge" tier;
  genuinely hard sheets carry a **`★ Harder`** marker on the sheet header and `science.html` (greyscale-safe, matching the question bank's `★`).
- Home page `worksheets/science.html` (3 columns, KS4 and KS3 lists, defaults to the signed-in student's year; `#ks3` / `#ks4` wins;
  course-aware: Single Award students see their versions; admins see everything with a "view as" switch). Old sheets: `worksheets/archive.html`.
- Files: `worksheets/<subject>/ks4|ks3/<code>_<name>.html` + `…_ms.html` flowing mark scheme; `_sa` (Single Award) twins alongside in `ks4/`.
  The home page was generated by Claude's builder (`home.js`, private) — hand edits are fine but say so in the commit.

## Codes (user, 20 Nov 2026) — no hyphens
- The code names every spec sub-topic the sheet covers: `3d` = topic 3(d); two sub-topics → both letters (`2ab`, `4cd`, `8bc`);
  a whole-topic sheet is just the number (biology `1`); reviews are just the topic number (not "1R"). Sheets sharing letters are numbered
  (`1e1`–`1e5`, `3a1`/`3a2`). Physics sub-topic (a) = units, ignored. `0` = skills. **Codes clash across subjects: ask which subject.**

## Rules
- **KS3: exactly 2 sides**, fundamentals. **KS4: covers everything**, 2 or 4 sides. Every topic ends with a review sheet. Hard skills are met
  twice (e.g. moles again after topic 2).
- **Greyscale (user, 22 Nov):** everything must read when printed in black and white — atoms carry symbols, ions + / −, chromatogram spots a
  letter + key, second graph line dashed, lines labelled. **No overlapping atoms** ("too messy").
- Fixed A4 pages with `css/print-sheet.css`; header `{code} · {title}`, `IGCSE Edexcel {Subject} · KS4|KS3`; spec line `.ps-spec` starting
  with the point numbers (read by `tools/spec/build_index.js`). Titles fit on one line. `<meta name="viewport" content="width=820">`.
- Fill pages properly: growing ruled lines, bigger diagrams/tables. Big open writing boxes read as too open — break them up.
- Order: recall → single-step → multi-step → unfamiliar; units; mark schemes step by step with a tip (answers green; biology blue).
  KS3: word banks, tick tables, match-ups, worked first rows. The user likes drawing and particle/electron understanding, fewer long answers.
- `/js/home-link.js` on every sheet and mark scheme.
- The teacher's own worksheets can be reused as ideas, rebuilt in our own words and numbers. **Never copy publisher or past-paper text or diagrams.**
- Limiting reactants and the Avogadro constant are not in the Double Award spec.
- KS3 leaves out: physics 1.10, 3.18, 3.19, 3.22, 4.14 calc, 5.7, 5.19, 5.21, 5.22, 7.7, 7.8, 8.6, standard form; chemistry 1.17, 1e2–1e5,
  electron part of 2.20, 2.35–2.36, 3.3–3.4, 4.5; biology SA:V calcs (2.16), symbol equations (2.19, 2.37), 2.89, 3.9, 3.30, 5.8, 5.12–5.14.
- Code-drawn diagrams can come from `interactive/spec-quiz/diagrams.js` and `interactive/cell-game/cells.js`.

## Single Award (KS4 only)
- No tags scattered through a sheet. **One Double-Award-only section → move it to the end** with `<span class="da">Double Award only</span>`.
  Otherwise make a Single Award twin `{code}_{name}_sa.html` (+ `_sa_ms.html`), 2 sides, one tag at the top of each version.
- Single Award sheets are friendlier: no challenge sections, no open explain boxes — gap fills with word banks, circle-the-word, tables,
  tick boxes, matching, diagrams to read, room for calculations.
- Sheets with no Single Award content are hidden from Single Award students (home page `SA_SKIP`).

## Visual & Icon Design Principles (Monoline SVG System)
Applies to all worksheets, booklets, covers, hub banners, and shared icons (`img/icons/`, `booklets/assets/covers/`):
- **Strictly within Edexcel IGCSE Double Award (`4SD0`):** every scientific motif must depict real, accurate Double Award science. Never use A-level motifs (e.g. aromatic benzene rings), Separate-only structures (e.g. DNA double-helix base-pair ladders — use plant cell `B2.2`, leaf `B2.18`, or 2×2 Punnett square `B3.22`), pop-culture clichés (tilted Rutherford oval orbits — use concentric circular shells `C1.15/P7.2`, prism refraction `P3.17`, transverse waves `P3.2`, or star + planet/comet orbits `P8.5`), or mismatched electron counts.
- **Mayer's Coherence Principle (no seductive details):** graphics must signal structure or teach, never clutter. Keep printable worksheets quiet — one compact `7.5 mm` subject emblem in the header and a monochrome warning triangle on mark-scheme tips, with zero decorative watermarks behind questions.
- **Consistent monoline SVG grids:** use pure rounded monoline line art (`fill="none"`, `stroke-linecap="round"`, `stroke-linejoin="round"`, zero gradients, zero OS emojis). Stick to two pixel-snapped grids: `24×24` (`2px` stroke, `2px` padding) for UI/cue icons, and `32×32` (`2.2px` stroke, `3px` padding) for subject emblems, optically balanced so circles/triangles match square visual weight.
- **Greyscale & photocopy-first print rules:** every printable emblem and cover must have a dedicated `-bw.svg` greyscale counterpart (`#111` / `#1a1a1a` ink on white or light `#f3f3f3` paper via `@media print` — never dark toner blocks). Keep physical printed stroke widths `≥ 0.35 mm` (`2.2px` on `32×32` at `7.5 mm` = `0.52 mm`) so lines never drop out on school photocopiers.
- **Accessibility:** always pair UI icons with visible text labels (`alt=""` on decorative pairings), rely on shape + negative space rather than colour alone, and keep `≥ 3:1` graphical contrast on screen (`≥ 7:1` for text headers).
