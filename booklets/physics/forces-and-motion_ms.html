★  Exam-style questions

X1 [6] any six:
  • hang the rubber band from a clamp stand; fix a metre rule vertically beside it
  • measure the original length with no load
  • add masses / weights one at a time (e.g. 100 g = 1 N)
  • measure the new length each time
  • calculate extension = new length − original length
  • read the ruler at eye level / use a pointer
  • remove the masses and repeat for each force; calculate a mean
  • plot a graph of extension against force
  • safety: goggles in case the band snaps; tray or cushion under the masses / don’t stand under them
Award marks for points, not for a list of equipment alone.
