How they work

Print

Printing links open your browser's print window with the booklet ready: cover, contents with page numbers, and questions with answer lines. Turn off “Headers and footers” for a clean copy.

Online

Write a first answer, then reveal the mark scheme. Your first answer locks, and anything you add afterwards is kept separately as what you learned.

Mark schemes

Point-by-point, like the real ones: more creditworthy points than marks, with notes on what to allow, reject and ignore.
