How Breadventure Land works
  • One world for everyone who plays. The other adventurers on the map are real players. Click a face to see who they are and where they're heading.
  • Plan, then sleep. Tap a glowing square to plan a move. Everyone's moves happen together at midnight, and the morning report tells you what happened.
  • Moves: 6 on your first day, then 2 a day, +1 for the day's first revision (a mission or today's flashcards). Unused moves bank, up to 4. A move is one step, or staying put to fight or explore again.
  • Same square, same fight. Players on a boss's square at the same move fight it together. Boss health carries over night to night and never goes back up.
  • Small monsters (orange bars) are your own: beat them on your own for coins. They come back two days later.
  • Health only comes back through revision: +2 per right mission answer, +1 per flashcard, +5 for a night resting in the Cakelab. Mana for spells comes from flashcards and missions. Knocked out = back to the Cakelab on a quarter of your Health; nothing lost.
  • Water can't be crossed except at the bridges. Fog: you only see 2 squares around anywhere you've been.
  • Rewards: coins from monsters and dungeons; when a boss falls, everyone who ever hit it gets coins and its item. They arrive next time you open the map.

Stats

  • Strength (damage) = Physics spec points secured + 2 per level + gear.
  • Health = Biology spec points secured + 3 per level.
  • Magic = Chemistry spec points secured + 1 per level. More Magic, more mana.
  • Level comes from XP, capped by spec points secured: 2 + 1 per 8.

The quest

  • Chapter 1: beat the Spore Lord, the Stone Golem and the Acid Hydra to win the three key pieces and free the Professor.
  • Chapter 2 wakes the Superbug, the Rust Wyrm and the Half-Life Horror.
  • The Ancient Gate, the Broken Bridge and the Abandoned Mine are for later chapters.
