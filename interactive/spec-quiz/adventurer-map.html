How the map works (design notes for teachers)
  • 2 moves a day. A move is one step to a neighbouring square, or staying put to fight or explore again. Unused moves bank, up to 4.
  • Everything happens at midnight. Next login shows the morning report. No reason to be online at night.
  • Same square = same team. Boss health carries over night to night, so a class can wear a boss down over a week. No fighting each other.
  • Losing is gentle. Health 0 = knocked out, wake up in the Common Room, nothing lost.
  • Water can't be crossed (sea, Lost Property Lake, the River Tangent) except at the two bridges, so routes matter.
  • Fog of war: you only see squares within 2 steps of somewhere you've been. Bosses show through the fog as shadows so there's always somewhere to head for. Exploring is its own reward.

Stats

  • Strength (damage) = Physics spec points secured + 2 per level + gear.
  • Health = Biology spec points secured + 3 per level.
  • Magic = Chemistry spec points secured + 1 per level. Spells come later.
  • Level comes from map XP but is capped by the quiz: 2 + 1 per 8 spec points secured. XP over the cap waits in the bank.

Room to grow (on the map)

  • The Broken Bridge (west coast): leads off the map; a future map or a class repair event.
  • The Abandoned Mine (Gravity Peaks): a later dungeon.
  • The Exam Hall Gate (east): portal to Map 2.
  • Separate Science Ferry (south coast): triple-science island.
  • The Mock Exam (north): world boss, asleep until before mocks.
