How Breadventure Land works
  • One world for everyone who plays. The other adventurers on the map are real players. Click a face to see who they are and where they're heading.
  • Moves happen straight away. Tap a glowing square (or any square you can see, for the whole way there). Small monsters fight you on the spot: each move is one question, right and you hit it, wrong and it hits you. Bosses wait for midnight: step onto one to make camp; each move you spend there is one attack, and everyone camped at the same boss fights it together. Walking away breaks camp and gives the moves back.
  • Moves: 6 on your first day, then 2 a day, +1 for the day's first revision (a mission or today's flashcards). Unused moves bank, up to 4. A move is one step, or staying put to fight or explore again.
  • Same square, same fight. Players on a boss's square at the same move fight it together, with a rally bonus of +10% damage for each ally. On the same square as another player you can tap them to patch them up (3 mana) or give them a brew. Each week has an event (shown under the quest).
  • Bosses: Boss health carries over night to night and never goes back up.
  • Small monsters (orange bars) are your own: beat them on your own for coins. They come back two days later.
  • Health only comes back through revision: +2 per right mission answer, +1 per flashcard, +5 for a night resting in the Cakelab. Mana for spells comes from flashcards and missions. Knocked out = back to the Cakelab on a quarter of your Health; nothing lost.
  • Water can't be crossed except at the bridges. Fog: you only see 2 squares around anywhere you've been.
  • New things open as you level up (and you level up by securing spec points): mana and spells at level 2, hats at 3, the Tuck Shop and weekly events at 4, dungeons at 5, the Abandoned Mine at 7. Locked places show a padlock and the level.
  • Dungeons are played awake. End a move at a dungeon door, then open the map and go in: three floors of questions on its theme, 3 right clears a floor, loot on each one (a brew and something to wear at the bottom). Once a day per dungeon.
  • Rewards: coins from monsters and dungeons; when a boss falls, everyone who ever hit it gets coins and its item. They arrive next time you open the map.

Stats

  • Strength (damage) = Physics spec points secured + 2 per level + gear.
  • Health = Biology spec points secured + 3 per level.
  • Magic = Chemistry spec points secured + 1 per level. More Magic, more mana.
  • Level comes from XP, capped by spec points secured: 2 + 1 per 8.

The quest

  • Chapter 1: beat the Spore Lord, the Stone Golem and the Acid Hydra to win the three key pieces and free the Professor.
  • Chapter 2 wakes the Superbug, the Rust Wyrm and the Half-Life Horror.
  • The Abandoned Mine is five floors deep and remembers how far down you got. Clear the bottom floor and its lift takes you to the Underworld: a second map under this one, with tougher monsters, two more dungeons and a boss of its own. Ride the lift from the mine (down) or the Mine Lift (up); it's free, but it cancels the moves you'd planned.
  • The Ancient Gate and the Broken Bridge are for later chapters.
