How it works

  • 2 moves a day, like Kingdom of Loathing's daily adventures but much smaller. A move is one step to a neighbouring square, or staying put to fight or explore again. Unused moves bank up to 4, so missing a day isn't a disaster but nobody can hoard.
  • Overnight, everything happens at once (at midnight). Moves you planned play out, bosses fight back, dungeons get explored, and the morning report tells the story with jokes. Nothing happens in real time, so there's no reason to be online at night.
  • Same square = same team. Everyone who ends the night on a boss square fights it together. Boss health carries over from night to night and is visible to all, so a class can wear down a boss over a week.
  • Losing is gentle. If your Health runs out you are “knocked out” and wake up in the Common Room. You lose nothing except the walk back.
  • Bosses drop loot for everyone who hit them that week: gear, brews and XP. Dungeons give smaller random finds and the odd fight.
  • Everyone is visible on the map as their little portrait, so you can see where the class is heading and pile in.
  • No fighting each other. Players only ever team up.

Brews (the temporary potions)

I'd call them Brews: short, chemistry-flavoured, and they fit “Chemistry gives Magic”. Alternatives: Tonics, Concoctions, Lab Snacks. A brew lasts a number of moves, not hours. Found on bosses and in dungeons, or bought at the Tuck Shop with coins from fights.

  • Stat points come from the quiz, not the map: each spec point you secure in a science adds 1 to its stat. Real revision is the only way to get stronger, so a student can't grind the map instead of learning.
  • Level comes from the map (XP from bosses and dungeons) but is capped by the quiz: your level cap is 2 + one level for every 8 spec points secured. Hit the cap and XP waits in the bank until you secure more points.
  • Gear can add small bonuses (+1 to +3) to a stat. The joke stats (Aura, Brain Cells, Vibes) stay jokes.
  • Weaker students still matter: team fights add everyone's damage together, every hit counts towards the shared loot, and luck plays a part.

Room to grow (shown on the map)

  • Fog round the edge (Uncharted): new squares can be revealed as the term goes on.
  • The Exam Hall Gate: a locked portal to Map 2 (e.g. next term's topics or a mocks map).
  • The Separate Science Ferry: closed for now; an island for triple-science content.
  • The Mock Exam: a world boss asleep at the top of the map, to wake for the whole year group before mocks.
  • Spells for Magic, class banners, a weekly boss rotation and seasonal events can slot in without changing the map.
