E  Oxidation, reduction and redox spec 2.20

Reminder: two definitions of redox
In terms of oxygen: oxidation = gain of oxygen · reduction = loss of oxygen.
In terms of electrons (OILRIG): Oxidation Is Loss of electrons · Reduction Is Gain of electrons.
A redox reaction has both happening together. The oxidising agent oxidises the other substance (and is itself reduced); the reducing agent reduces the other substance (and is itself oxidised).
