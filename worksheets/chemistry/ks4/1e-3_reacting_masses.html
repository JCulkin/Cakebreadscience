Reminder: three steps
1 Moles of the substance you know: mass ÷ Mr.   2 Use the big numbers in the balanced equation to find moles of the substance you want.   3 Mass you want = moles × Mr.
Ar: H 1 · C 12 · N 14 · O 16 · Na 23 · Mg 24 · S 32 · Cl 35.5 · K 39 · Ca 40 · Fe 56 · Cu 63.5 · Br 80
Worked example · what mass of magnesium oxide forms when 6 g of magnesium burns?
2Mg + O2 → 2MgO
1 moles Mg = 6 ÷ 24 = 0.25 mol
2 2 Mg : 2 MgO is 1 : 1, so 0.25 mol MgO
3 Mr MgO = 40; mass = 0.25 × 40 = 10 g

C  Percentage yield

The theoretical yield is the mass you calculate from the equation. The actual yield is the mass you really get. It is almost always less.
% yield = actual yieldtheoretical yield × 100

How it works
