F  Copper(II) sulfate crystals

F1
In the table order: 3, 6, 1, 5, 2, 4
Warm acid → add oxide to excess → filter → evaporate until crystals start → cool → filter and dry.
F2
So all the acid reacts (no acid left in the salt). You can see black solid left that won't dissolve.
F3
The crystals are hydrated: they need water of crystallisation. Heating to dryness would give white powder (anhydrous) or spit.
F4
Black solid + colourless acid → blue solution (blue crystals).

G  Plan it

G1
Use hydrochloric acid. Add zinc carbonate to warm acid until fizzing stops and some solid is left (excess). Filter off the excess. Heat the filtrate until crystals start to form, leave to cool and crystallise, filter and dry the crystals between filter papers. zinc carbonate + hydrochloric acid → zinc chloride + water + carbon dioxide.
