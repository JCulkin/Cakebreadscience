B21. Fe2O3 + CO
Correct: Fe2O3 + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO2.
They changed a small number: CO became CO3, which is a different substance.
Never change the small subscript numbers in a formula (changing H2O to H2O2 makes a different compound) — only put big coefficients in front.

F2. Mass goes down
No atoms are lost: if the gas were collected, the total mass would be the same.
Carbon dioxide gas is made and escapes from the open flask.
F3. Mass goes up
The magnesium oxide weighs more than the magnesium.
Magnesium combines with oxygen from the air; the oxygen atoms add mass.
Watch brackets in Mr: in Ca(OH)2 the 2 multiplies both O and H (40 + 2×16 + 2×1 = 74). Mr has no units.
