D  True or false?

statementanswer
1. Elements are arranged in order of atomic number.True.
2. A row of the Periodic Table is called a group.False: a row is a period; a column is a group.
3. Metals are on the left of the Periodic Table.True (and in the middle).
4. Most elements are non-metals.False: most elements are metals.
5. Hydrogen is a non-metal.True.
