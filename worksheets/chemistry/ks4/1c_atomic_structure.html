Reminder
An atom has a tiny nucleus containing protons and neutrons, surrounded by electrons in shells. The atomic number (bottom number) = number of protons. The mass number (top number) = protons + neutrons. Atoms have no overall charge, so number of electrons = number of protons. Isotopes are atoms of the same element with different numbers of neutrons.

D  Relative atomic mass, Ar a weighted mean of the isotopes

Think of it like this
A bag holds 90 small balls of mass 2 g and 10 large balls of mass 3 g. The mean mass of a ball is (90 × 2 + 10 × 3) ÷ 100 = 2.1 g: closer to 2 g because most balls are small.
Worked example · chlorine: 75% chlorine-35 and 25% chlorine-37
Ar = (35 × 75 + 37 × 25) ÷ 100 = (2625 + 925) ÷ 100 = 35.5
