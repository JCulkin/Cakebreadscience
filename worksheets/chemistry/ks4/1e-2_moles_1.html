Reminder
The mole (mol) is the unit for amount of substance. One mole of any substance has a mass in grams equal to its Mr (or Ar for an element made of atoms). One mole of carbon is 12 g; one mole of water (Mr 18) is 18 g.
moles = mass (g)Mr
Ar: H 1 · C 12 · N 14 · O 16 · Na 23 · Mg 24 · S 32 · Cl 35.5 · K 39 · Ca 40 · Fe 56 · Cu 63.5 · Br 80
Worked example · how many moles in 36 g of water?
Mr of H2O = 2 × 1 + 16 = 18  →  moles = 36 ÷ 18 = 2 mol
