Percentage yield = (actual yield ÷ theoretical yield) × 100 — always ≤ 100%. Round only at the final step and match the significant figures given in the question.

Spot the mistake
They divided Mr by mass. Moles of Mg = 4.8 ÷ 24 = 0.2 mol → 0.2 mol MgO → 0.2 × 40 = 8.0 g

Four steps every time: (1) moles of known = mass ÷ Mᵣ, (2) mole ratio from the balanced equation, (3) mass of unknown = moles × Mᵣ, (4) check your unit. Percentage yield is always (actual ÷ theoretical) × 100.
