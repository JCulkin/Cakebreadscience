A4. Water vs hydrogen + oxygen (any two)
Water has different properties (liquid at room temperature, does not burn); the elements are in a fixed ratio (2 H : 1 O); it can only be split by a chemical reaction; energy is given out when it forms.

A5. Drawings
Element: every particle the same, e.g. ○○ pairs. Compound: every particle the same, each made of ○ and ● joined. Mixture: ○○ (or ●●) molecules and separate ○● molecules, not joined.

D7. How it separates
The solvent (mobile phase) moves up the paper (stationary phase). Dyes that are more soluble in the solvent spend more time in the mobile phase and travel further, so the dyes separate.
D8. Method
Pencil does not dissolve in the solvent (ink would run). Solvent below the line so the spots do not dissolve into the solvent in the beaker. Remove before the top so you can mark the solvent front (and measure Rf). Lid stops the solvent evaporating.
D9. Banned dye
Put a spot of the food colouring and a spot of pure Z on a pencil start line. Stand the paper in solvent below the line; let the solvent rise, then dry. If the food colouring has a spot at the same height (same Rf) as Z, it contains Z.
