Reminder: three structures
Giant ionic: a lattice of + and − ions held by strong electrostatic attractions in all directions.   Simple molecular: small molecules; strong covalent bonds inside each molecule but weak intermolecular forces between molecules.   Giant covalent: millions of atoms all joined by strong covalent bonds.
To conduct, a substance needs charged particles that can move (ions or delocalised electrons).
