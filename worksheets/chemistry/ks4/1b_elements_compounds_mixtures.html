Reminder
An element is made of only one type of atom. A compound is two or more elements chemically joined (bonded) in fixed proportions. A mixture is two or more substances that are not chemically joined, so they can be separated by physical methods.
In chemistry a pure substance is a single element or a single compound. A pure substance melts and boils at one fixed temperature; a mixture melts and boils over a range of temperatures.
