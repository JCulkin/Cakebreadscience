C  Spot the mistakes

Question: what mass of carbon dioxide forms when 25 g of calcium carbonate decomposes? (CaCO3 → CaO + CO2)
Student: Mr CaCO3 = 40 + 12 + 16 = 68. Moles = 25 ÷ 68 = 0.37. Mass of CO2 = 0.37 × 28 = 10.3 g.
