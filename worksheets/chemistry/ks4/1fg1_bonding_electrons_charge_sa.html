Reminder: where charge comes from
Atoms have equal numbers of protons (+) and electrons (−). Metals (Groups 1, 2, 3) lose electrons to make positive ions; non-metals (Groups 5, 6, 7) gain electrons to make negative ions. The group number tells you how many outer electrons there are: Group 1 loses 1, Group 7 gains 1, Group 6 gains 2, Group 5 gains 3.
