A4. Spot the mistake
Solid NaCl does not conduct because its ions are fixed in the lattice and cannot move.
In ionic compounds the charge is carried by ions, not electrons.
A6. Drawings
Solid: ions touching in a regular alternating pattern (● next to ○). Molten: ions still close together but randomly arranged, able to move past each other.
A5. MgO vs NaCl
More energy is needed to overcome it.
The ions in MgO have bigger charges (2+ and 2−), so the electrostatic attraction between them is stronger.

C2. Diamond
Many strong bonds must be broken to melt it or scratch it.
Each carbon is joined to 4 others by strong covalent bonds in a giant structure.
C3. Graphite conducts
In diamond all 4 outer electrons are in bonds, so none are free.
Each carbon forms only 3 bonds, so one outer electron per carbon is delocalised and free to move through the layers.
C4. Soft and slippery
The layers are held together only by weak forces, so they slide over each other easily.
C5. C60
Diamond is giant covalent: strong covalent bonds must break.
C60 is made of separate molecules: melting only overcomes the weak intermolecular forces between them.

F  Mark the answer

What is wrong
Boiling does not break covalent bonds; it overcomes the intermolecular forces between molecules.
Better answer
Ethane has a larger Mr (30 vs 28), so the intermolecular forces between its molecules are a little stronger and need more energy to overcome. So ethane has the higher boiling point.
