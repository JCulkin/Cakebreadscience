Reminder: the particle model
All matter is made of tiny particles that are always moving (or vibrating). In each state the particles have a different arrangement, movement and energy. Heating gives particles more energy; changes of state happen when the particles get enough energy to overcome (or are held by) the forces of attraction between them.
Drawing tip: draw every particle the same size. Liquid particles must still touch.
