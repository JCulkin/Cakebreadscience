B  Sodium chloride

B1
It is the electron that sodium lost: it moved from the sodium atom to the chlorine atom.
B2
They have opposite charges (+ and −), which attract strongly (electrostatic attraction).

D1

HCl: one shared pair; Cl has 3 more pairs
Cl2: one shared pair; 3 more pairs on each Cl
Ionic = metal + non-metal (electrons transferred, forming ions). Covalent = non-metal + non-metal (electrons shared in pairs).

E  Ionic or covalent?

E1
potassium bromide: ionic · methane: covalent · carbon dioxide: covalent · calcium oxide: ionic
E2
Metal + non-metal → ionic. Non-metals only → covalent.
