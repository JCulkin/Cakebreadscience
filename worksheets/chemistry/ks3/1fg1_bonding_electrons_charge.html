Reminder
Atoms lose or gain electrons to get a full outer shell. Metals lose electrons → positive ions. Non-metals gain electrons → negative ions. Oppositely charged ions attract: that is an ionic bond. Two non-metal atoms can instead share a pair of electrons: a covalent bond.
