Reminder
Atoms are not made or destroyed in a reaction, so a balanced equation has the same number of each atom on both sides. Balance by writing big numbers in front of formulae, never by changing the small numbers. Relative formula mass (Mr) = add up the relative atomic masses (Ar) of all the atoms in the formula.
