E  Making copper sulfate crystals number the steps 1–6

Filter the mixture to remove the excess copper(II) oxide.
Filter off the crystals and dry them between filter papers.
Warm some dilute sulfuric acid in a beaker.
Leave the solution to cool and crystallise.
Add copper(II) oxide a spatula at a time, stirring, until no more dissolves (some black solid is left).
Heat the blue filtrate in an evaporating basin until crystals start to form at the edge.
