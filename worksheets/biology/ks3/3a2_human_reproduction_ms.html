F  True or false?

Sperm are made in the ovaries.False: in the testes.
Fertilisation happens in the oviduct.True.
The baby grows in the uterus.True.
The mother’s blood mixes with the baby’s blood.False: they stay separate; substances diffuse across.
Testosterone makes the voice deepen.True.
Remember the route: egg released from ovary → fertilised in the oviduct → grows into a baby in the uterus.
