D2. P is a bacterium
It has no nucleus: its DNA is a loop in the cytoplasm (with small rings called plasmids).
D3. How fungi feed
They release enzymes onto dead or living material, which digest it outside the fungus; then they absorb the small food molecules.

Plasmodium → malaria · Pneumococcus → pneumonia · influenza virus → flu · HIV → AIDS
F3. Viruses are not living (any two)
They are not made of cells; they can only reproduce inside a living cell; they do not feed, respire or grow on their own.
F4. Useful bacteria
Making yoghurt (Lactobacillus); also cheese, or breaking down dead material.
