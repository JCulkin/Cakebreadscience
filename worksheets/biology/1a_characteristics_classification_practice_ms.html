B  Living or not? answers

B1. A car
Moves and "uses fuel", but it does not grow, reproduce, respond or excrete by itself, and is not made of cells.
not living
B2. A candle flame
Uses oxygen, gives off waste and can "grow", but it does not reproduce, has no cells and cannot control its internal conditions.
not living
B3. A virus
It can only reproduce inside a living cell. On its own it does not feed, respire, excrete or grow, and it has no cell structure.
not a living organism
B4. A dry seed
Looks dead, but it respires very slowly and can grow and reproduce once it germinates.
living
To count as living, an organism must show all eight characteristics at some point in its life. Showing one or two (a car moves) is not enough.
