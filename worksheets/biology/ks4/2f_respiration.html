Reminder
Respiration is a chemical reaction in every living cell (aerobic in the mitochondria) that releases energy from glucose to make ATP (adenosine triphosphate). ATP provides energy for muscle contraction, building proteins, active transport and keeping warm. Respiration is not breathing.
