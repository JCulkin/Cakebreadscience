Reminder
Diffusion: the net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration. Osmosis: the net movement of water molecules from a dilute solution to a more concentrated solution, through a partially permeable membrane. Active transport: the movement of particles against a concentration gradient (low → high), using energy from respiration.
