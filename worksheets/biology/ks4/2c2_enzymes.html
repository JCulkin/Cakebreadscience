Reminder
An enzyme is a protein that acts as a biological catalyst: it speeds up a reaction without being used up. The substrate fits into the enzyme's active site, which has a matching shape (like a key in a lock). High temperatures or the wrong pH change the shape of the active site, so the substrate no longer fits: the enzyme is denatured.
