1b · Classifying Organisms

IGCSE Edexcel Biology · KS4
1.1–1.4 · classifying unfamiliar organisms, saprotrophic nutrition, scale conversions, pathogens and viruses
Name:
Eukaryotes (nucleus): plants (multicellular, cellulose walls, chloroplasts, starch), animals (multicellular, no walls, glycogen), fungi (chitin walls, hyphae or single-celled yeast, saprotrophic, glycogen), protoctists (mostly single-celled). Prokaryotes: bacteria (no nucleus, circular chromosome + plasmids). Viruses: non-living particles (protein coat + DNA/RNA, reproduce only inside host cells).

C  How fungi feed: saprotrophic nutrition

C1. Number the steps 1–4 in order to show how Mucor feeds on bread.
Small, soluble molecules are absorbed into the hyphae.
Hyphae grow over and into the bread.
Enzymes break large insoluble food molecules into small soluble ones outside the fungus.
Hyphae secrete digestive enzymes onto the bread.
C2. Explain why this process is called extracellular digestion.
