D13
They break down dead organisms and waste, releasing nutrients (and carbon dioxide) back into the soil and air to be used again.
Food-web arrows always point in the direction of energy transfer (from eaten → eater), never "who eats whom". Predators feeding on prey is predation (a biotic factor).

E3

G  Moving substances

G1
Carbon compounds in the grass are eaten and digested by the rabbit and become part of its body; the fox eats the rabbit. Respiration in all of them (and decomposers breaking down dead bodies and waste) releases carbon dioxide into the air.
