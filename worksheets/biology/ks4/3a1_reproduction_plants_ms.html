F  Food stores in seeds

F1
day 5
F2
The seedling cannot photosynthesise yet (no leaves above the soil). It uses its food store (starch) for respiration, releasing energy for growth, so mass is lost as carbon dioxide and water.
F3
Leaves have grown, so the seedling photosynthesises faster than it respires and makes new glucose / biomass.

G  Asexual reproduction

G1
A side shoot grows along the ground; where it touches the soil it grows roots and a new plant, which later separates. Made by mitosis from one parent, so genetically identical.
G2
In the table order: 3, 5, 1, 4, 2
Cut shoot → remove lower leaves → rooting powder → compost → bag.
G3
Advantage: quick, cheap, every plant has the parent's good features. Disadvantage: no variation, so one disease or change can kill them all.
