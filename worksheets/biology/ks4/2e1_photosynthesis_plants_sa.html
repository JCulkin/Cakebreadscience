D  Testing a leaf for starch

D1. Number the steps 1–4 in order.
Heat the leaf in ethanol (in a hot water bath) to remove the green chlorophyll.
Add iodine solution — starch turns it blue-black.
Dip the leaf in boiling water to soften it and stop reactions.
Rinse the leaf in warm water to soften it again.
D2. Circle the right word: Ethanol is heated in a hot water bath, not over a flame, because it is acidic · flammable · heavy.
