A  Lock and key

A2. Stages
1: the substrate collides with the active site, which has a complementary shape. 2: the substrate fits into the active site (enzyme–substrate complex) and reacts. 3: the products leave; the enzyme is unchanged.
A3. Specific
The active site has a particular shape; only a substrate with the matching (complementary) shape fits into it.
A4. Used again
The enzyme is not changed by the reaction; its active site is free for another substrate molecule.
A5. Biological catalyst
A substance made by living things that speeds up a reaction without being used up.
A6. Examples (any two)
Respiration, photosynthesis, protein synthesis (making proteins), digestion (breaking down food).

B4
