Aerobic respiration happens mainly in the mitochondria and transfers energy into ATP (adenosine triphosphate). After hard exercise, extra oxygen is needed to repay the oxygen debt and break down lactic acid.

It releases much less energy per glucose and lactic acid builds up, causing fatigue/cramp; it must be broken down afterwards (oxygen debt).
The heart and lungs cannot supply oxygen fast enough, so muscles release extra energy without oxygen.
B3
Respiration does not "make" energy — it releases energy from glucose; and plants do respire (all living cells respire day and night).
ATP transfers the released energy to power cell processes such as muscle contraction and protein synthesis.
