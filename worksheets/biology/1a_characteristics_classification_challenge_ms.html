1. Mystery organisms [6 marks]
Decide which group each organism belongs to: plant, animal, fungus, protoctist, bacterium, or virus. Give one piece of evidence from the card.
Organism A
Lives in pond water. Just one cell, with a nucleus and chloroplasts.
Organism B
Grows on old bread as a fuzzy mat of thin threads. Cell walls made of chitin. Releases enzymes onto the bread, then absorbs the products.
Organism C
A tiny single cell with no nucleus. Its DNA is one loop, plus some small extra rings.
Organism D
Made of many cells with no cell walls. Stores carbohydrate as glycogen and moves from place to place.
Organism E
Made of many cells with cellulose cell walls. Stores starch and makes its own food using light.
Organism F
Smaller than any bacterium. A protein coat around RNA. Can only make copies of itself inside a host cell.

3. How a fungus feeds [3 marks]
(a) Correct order: hyphae grow into food → release enzymes onto food → enzymes break down large molecules outside the fungus → small soluble molecules absorbed [2] (1 mark if one pair is swapped)
(b) Digestion happens outside the cells, on the food itself (extracellular), and the products are absorbed afterwards [1]
(a) numbers down the table: 4, 1, 3, 2

5. Tobacco mosaic virus [3 marks]
Fewer chloroplasts, so less light is absorbed / less photosynthesis [1] → less glucose made [1] → less glucose for respiration and for making new cells, so less growth [1]
fewer chloroplasts → less photosynthesis → less glucose → less growth
6. Is a virus alive? [4 marks]
Any four:
• viruses can only reproduce inside living (host) cells, not on their own [1]
• they do not feed / respire / excrete / grow / respond by themselves (1 mark each, max 2) [2]
• they have no cell structure, just a protein coat and DNA or RNA [1]
• living things show all the characteristics, so the student is wrong: viruses are not living organisms [1]
The student is wrong: reproducing (only inside cells) is just one characteristic.
