3. How a fungus feeds [3 marks]
  1. Number the steps 1–4 to show how Mucor feeds by saprotrophic nutrition.
Small, soluble molecules are absorbed into the hyphae.
Hyphae grow over and into the food.
Enzymes break large food molecules into small ones, outside the fungus.
Hyphae release digestive enzymes onto the food.
  1. This is called extracellular digestion. Explain why.
