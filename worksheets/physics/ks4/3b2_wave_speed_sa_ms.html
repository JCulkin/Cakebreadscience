B  More practice

1. f = 25 Hz, λ = 0.4 m
v = f × λ = 25 × 0.4
v = 10 m/s
2. λ = 0.68 m, v = 340 m/s
f = v ÷ λ = 340 ÷ 0.68
f = 500 Hz
3. Buoy: 12 waves in 60 s, 5 m apart
(a) f = 12 ÷ 60 = 0.2 Hz
(b) v = f × λ = 0.2 × 5
(a) f = 0.2 Hz   (b) v = 1 m/s
Frequency = waves per second, so divide the number of waves by the time.
4. Frequency doubles
The wavelength halves.
λ = v ÷ f; v stays the same, so doubling f halves λ.

C  Waves in context

1. Sound: v = 340 m/s, f = 170 Hz
λ = v ÷ f = 340 ÷ 170
λ = 2 m
2. Water: λ = 2 m, v = 3 m/s
f = v ÷ λ = 3 ÷ 2
f = 1.5 Hz
3. Earthquake: f = 2 Hz, λ = 4000 m
v = f × λ = 2 × 4000
v = 8000 m/s
4. Rope: 20 waves in 4 s, λ = 0.8 m
f = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 Hz
v = f × λ = 5 × 0.8
f = 5 Hz,   v = 4 m/s
Step 1's answer feeds into step 2. If f is wrong, v will be too.

E  Convert first, then calculate

1. Radio: 98.5 MHz
f = 98.5 MHz = 9.85 × 10⁷ Hz
λ = v ÷ f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 9.85 × 10⁷
λ = 3.05 m (3.0 m is fine)
2. Sound: 0.5 kHz, 340 m/s
f = 0.5 kHz = 500 Hz
λ = v ÷ f = 340 ÷ 500
λ = 0.68 m
Convert kHz to Hz first: × 1000.
3. Wi-Fi: 2.4 GHz
f = 2.4 GHz = 2.4 × 10⁹ Hz
λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2.4 × 10⁹ = 0.125 m
λ = 12.5 cm
The question asked for cm, so convert at the end: 0.125 × 100.
4. Ripples: 1.5 cm, 8 Hz
λ = 1.5 cm = 0.015 m
v = f × λ = 8 × 0.015
v = 0.12 m/s
If you got 12, you forgot to convert cm to m.

F  Spot the mistake

1. Sound, 2 kHz
Mistake: kHz was not converted to Hz.
2 kHz = 2000 Hz
λ = 340 ÷ 2000
λ = 0.17 m
2. v = 6 m/s, λ = 3 m
Mistake: wrong rearrangement. To find f you divide, not multiply.
f = v ÷ λ = 6 ÷ 3
f = 2 Hz
3. f = 50 Hz, λ = 20 cm
Mistake: cm was not converted to m.
20 cm = 0.2 m
v = 50 × 0.2
v = 10 m/s
