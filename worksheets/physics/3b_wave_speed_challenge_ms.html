Questions 1–3

1. Comparing waves (4 marks)
(a) λ = v ÷ f, so λA = (3v) ÷ (2f) = 1.5 × λB [1]
    λA = 1.5 × 6.0 = 9.0 cm [1]
(b) The wavelength halves. [1]
    f = 1 ÷ T, so halving T doubles f; v is unchanged, so λ = v ÷ f halves. [1]
(a) 9.0 cm   (b) λ halves (3.0 cm)
Don't put numbers in for v and f; work with the ratios.
2. Satellite TV (5 marks)
(a) f = 12 GHz = 1.2 × 10¹⁰ Hz; λ = 3.0 × 10⁸ ÷ 1.2 × 10¹⁰ [1]
    = 0.025 m = 2.5 cm [1]
(b) 3.6 × 10⁴ km = 3.6 × 10⁷ m; up and down = 7.2 × 10⁷ m [1]
    t = d ÷ v = 7.2 × 10⁷ ÷ 3.0 × 10⁸ = 0.24 s [1]
(c) The question goes up and down (0.24 s), then the reply goes up and down again (0.24 s): about 0.48 s in total. [1]
(a) 2.5 cm   (b) 0.24 s
km → m: × 1000, so 3.6 × 10⁴ km = 3.6 × 10⁷ m.
3. Oscilloscope (6 marks)
(a) 2.5 waves fill 10 divisions, so 1 wave = 4 divisions [1]
    T = 4 × 2 ms = 8 ms = 0.008 s [1]
(b) f = 1 ÷ T = 1 ÷ 0.008 = 125 Hz [1]
(c) λ = v ÷ f = 340 ÷ 125 [1] = 2.72 m [1]
(d) f doubles, so T halves: 4 ÷ 2 = 2 divisions [1]
(a) 0.008 s  (b) 125 Hz  (c) 2.7 m  (d) 2
Count divisions for a whole number of waves, then divide. Don't count one wave by eye.

Questions 4–6

4. Approaching storm (6 marks)
(a) d = v × t = 340 × 4.5 [1] = 1530 m [1]
(b) t = 1530 ÷ 3.0 × 10⁸ = 5.1 × 10⁻⁶ s [1]
    This is tiny compared with 4.5 s (and her reaction time), so the light arrives "instantly". [1]
(c) New distance = 340 × 3.0 = 1020 m; storm moved 1530 − 1020 = 510 m [1]
    v = 510 ÷ 120 = 4.25 m/s [1]
(a) 1530 m  (b) 5.1 × 10⁻⁶ s  (c) 4.3 m/s
2 minutes = 120 s. Using 2 gives 255 m/s.
5. Ultrasound scan (7 marks)
(a) λ = v ÷ f = 1540 ÷ 2.0 × 10⁶ = 7.7 × 10⁻⁴ m [1] = 0.77 mm [1]
(b) 65 μs = 6.5 × 10⁻⁵ s; d = 1540 × 6.5 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.100 m [1]
    That is there and back, so depth = 0.100 ÷ 2 [1] = 0.050 m = 5.0 cm [1]
(c) Number of waves = f × t = 2.0 × 10⁶ × 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ = 5 [1]
    Pulse length = 5 × 0.77 mm = 3.85 mm [1]
(a) 0.77 mm  (b) 5.0 cm  (c) 5 waves, 3.9 mm
μ (micro) = × 10⁻⁶. Forgetting to halve the echo gives 10 cm.
6. Locating an earthquake (5 marks)
(a) Time for S-waves − time for P-waves = 50 s:
    d ÷ 4.5 − d ÷ 8.0 = 50 [1]
    d × (0.2222 − 0.125) = 50, so d × 0.0972 = 50 [1]
    d = 50 ÷ 0.0972 = 514 km [1]
(b) λ = v ÷ f = 8.0 ÷ 2.0 [1] = 4.0 km [1]
(a) ≈ 510 km  (b) 4.0 km
Keep km and km/s together: no conversion is needed here.
