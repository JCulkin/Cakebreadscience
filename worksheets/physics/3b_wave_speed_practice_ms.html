B  Frequency and time period

1. T = 0.5 s
f = 1 ÷ T = 1 ÷ 0.5
f = 2 Hz
2. f = 50 Hz
T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ 50
T = 0.02 s
3. Buoy: 12 bobs in 60 s
(a) f = 12 ÷ 60
(b) T = 1 ÷ 0.2  (or 60 ÷ 12)
(a) f = 0.2 Hz   (b) T = 5 s
Frequency = waves per second, so divide the number of waves by the time.
4. Which has the shorter period?
Wave X.
T = 1 ÷ f, so a bigger frequency means a smaller time period. More waves pass each second, so each wave takes less time.

C  Waves in context

1. Sound: v = 340 m/s, f = 170 Hz
λ = v ÷ f = 340 ÷ 170
λ = 2 m
2. Water: λ = 2 m, v = 3 m/s
f = v ÷ λ = 3 ÷ 2
f = 1.5 Hz
3. Earthquake: f = 2 Hz, λ = 4000 m
v = f × λ = 2 × 4000
v = 8000 m/s
4. Rope: T = 0.25 s, λ = 0.8 m
f = 1 ÷ 0.25 = 4 Hz
v = f × λ = 4 × 0.8
f = 4 Hz,   v = 3.2 m/s
Step 1's answer feeds into step 2. If f is wrong, v will be too.

E  Convert first, then calculate

1. Radio: 98.5 MHz
f = 98.5 MHz = 9.85 × 10⁷ Hz
λ = v ÷ f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 9.85 × 10⁷
λ = 3.05 m (3.0 m is fine)
2. Red light: 650 nm
λ = 650 nm = 6.5 × 10⁻⁷ m
f = v ÷ λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 6.5 × 10⁻⁷
f = 4.6 × 10¹⁴ Hz
Use the ×10ˣ (EXP) button on your calculator, not × 10 ^.
3. Wi-Fi: 2.4 GHz
f = 2.4 GHz = 2.4 × 10⁹ Hz
λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2.4 × 10⁹ = 0.125 m
λ = 12.5 cm
The question asked for cm, so convert at the end: 0.125 × 100.
4. Ripples: 1.5 cm, 8 Hz
λ = 1.5 cm = 0.015 m
v = f × λ = 8 × 0.015
v = 0.12 m/s
If you got 12, you forgot to convert cm to m.

F  Spot the mistake

1. Sound, 2 kHz
Mistake: kHz was not converted to Hz.
2 kHz = 2000 Hz
λ = 340 ÷ 2000
λ = 0.17 m
2. v = 6 m/s, λ = 3 m
Mistake: wrong rearrangement. To find f you divide, not multiply.
f = v ÷ λ = 6 ÷ 3
f = 2 Hz
3. T = 20 ms
Mistake: ms was not converted to s.
20 ms = 0.02 s
f = 1 ÷ 0.02
f = 50 Hz

G  Challenge

1. Sonar
(a) The pulse goes down AND back up:
   distance = 2 × 600 = 1200 m
   v = d ÷ t = 1200 ÷ 0.8 = 1500 m/s
(b) f = 2.4 kHz = 2400 Hz
   λ = v ÷ f = 1500 ÷ 2400
(a) v = 1500 m/s   (b) λ = 0.625 m
Using 600 m gives 750 m/s, which is half the right answer. Always ask: does the wave go there and back?
2. Beach waves
(a) f = 15 ÷ 30 = 0.5 Hz
(b) T = 1 ÷ 0.5 = 2 s
(c) λ = 4 m (crest to crest)
   v = f × λ = 0.5 × 4 = 2 m/s
(d) t = d ÷ v = 50 ÷ 2
(a) 0.5 Hz  (b) 2 s  (c) 2 m/s  (d) 25 s
The distance between two crests IS the wavelength.
