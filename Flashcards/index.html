📖 How It Works

Spaced Repetition

Cards come back at longer intervals the better you know them, so you spend time on what you forget and less on what you already know.

Studying a Card

Tap a card (or press Space) to reveal the answer, then rate how well you knew it (1–4):

  • 1 · Again — Got it wrong. The card comes back again later in this session, and resets to 1 day.
  • 2 · Hard — Struggled. Stays in learning with a short interval (doesn't wipe your card back to unseen).
  • 3 · Good — Got it right with some thought. Interval grows (1d → 6d → longer).
  • 4 · Easy — Knew it straight away. Jumps to a longer interval.

Tapped the wrong button? Press ↩ Undo last (or Z) to step back.

Covered in Class & Study Ahead

When your teacher has ticked your year group's covered topics, flashcards automatically show only what you have covered in class (including inside Study by topic or sub-topic). Tick Study ahead (all topics) whenever you want to unlock topics you haven't done in lessons yet.

Optional Decks

Physics Equations and Examiner Key Words start turned off (greyed out) so your daily count stays focused on core Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. Click either deck to turn it on whenever you want to practise equations or mark-scheme vocabulary — and turn it off again anytime.

Card Stages

  • Unseen — Not studied yet.
  • Learning — Studied, review interval under 21 days.
  • Mastered — Review interval 21+ days.

Breadventure Land

When signed in, flashcards power your adventurer in Breadventure Land:

  • Every due card: +2 XP (up to 60 a day), +1 Health and +1 mana for Breadventure Land, and a coin every 5 cards.
  • Review 15 cards in a day (or all due cards if fewer) for +1 move and a loot drop.
  • Rate a card Good or Easy on two different days to secure it (flashcards count for up to half of a spec point; prove the rest with Practice or Questing).
  • Cards rated Again or Hard flag their spec point for your suggested missions tomorrow.
